# Risk Management & Drawdown Analysis

## Maximum Drawdown (MDD)
Maximum drawdown is the maximum observed loss from a peak to a trough of a portfolio, before a new peak is attained.
- **Formula**: `MDD = (Trough Value - Peak Value) / Peak Value`
- **Importance**: MDD is an indicator of downside risk over a specified time period.

## Position Sizing Rule (1-2% Rule)
Professional traders recommend never risking more than 1% to 2% of total account capital on any single trade setup.
- **Formula**: `Position Size = (Account Value * Risk %) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)`

## Stop-Loss Orders
A stop-loss order is an order placed with a broker to buy or sell a specific stock once the stock reaches a certain price. Stop-loss orders are designed to limit an investor's loss on a security position.

## Volatility
Volatility represents how large an asset's prices swing around the mean price. High volatility implies high risk and high reward opportunity.
