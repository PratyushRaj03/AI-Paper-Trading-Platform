# Financial Ratios & Valuation Metrics

## Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio
The Price-to-Earnings ratio evaluates a company's current share price relative to its per-share earnings.
- **Formula**: `P/E = Market Value per Share / Earnings per Share (EPS)`
- **Interpretation**: A high P/E implies investors expect higher earnings growth in the future compared to low P/E companies.

## Sharpe Ratio
The Sharpe ratio measures the risk-adjusted return of an investment portfolio.
- **Formula**: `Sharpe Ratio = (Portfolio Return - Risk-Free Rate) / Standard Deviation of Portfolio`
- **Interpretation**:
  - `< 1.0`: Sub-optimal risk-adjusted return
  - `1.0 to 1.99`: Good
  - `2.0 to 2.99`: Very Good
  - `>= 3.0`: Outstanding

## Profit Factor
Profit Factor is the ratio of gross profits to gross losses over a specified trading period.
- **Formula**: `Profit Factor = Total Gross Profits / Total Gross Losses`
- A profit factor above 1.5 indicates a robust winning strategy.

## Earnings Per Share (EPS)
EPS represents the portion of a company's profit allocated to each outstanding share of common stock.
- **Formula**: `EPS = (Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / End-of-Period Common Shares Outstanding`
