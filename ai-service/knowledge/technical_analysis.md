# Technical Analysis Basics

## Support and Resistance
- **Support**: A price level where a downtrend can be expected to pause due to a concentration of demand or buying interest.
- **Resistance**: A price level where an uptrend can be expected to pause temporarily, due to a concentration of supply or selling interest.

## Moving Averages
- **Simple Moving Average (SMA)**: The average price over a specific number of time periods (e.g., 50-day or 200-day SMA).
- **Golden Cross**: Occurs when a short-term moving average (e.g., 50-day) crosses above a long-term moving average (e.g., 200-day), signaling a strong bullish trend.
- **Death Cross**: Occurs when a short-term moving average crosses below a long-term moving average, signaling a bearish trend.

## Relative Strength Index (RSI)
RSI is a momentum indicator that measures the magnitude of recent price changes to evaluate overbought or oversold conditions.
- **> 70**: Overbought (potential sell signal or pullback)
- **< 30**: Oversold (potential buy signal or bounce)
