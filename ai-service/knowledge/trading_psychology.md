# Trading Psychology & Behavioral Insights

## Revenge Trading
Revenge trading occurs when a trader experiences a significant loss and immediately opens aggressive new trades to win back the lost money. This emotional response frequently leads to oversized position sizing, ignored stop-losses, and compounded losses.

## Loss Aversion Bias
Loss aversion is a cognitive bias where the psychological pain of losing is twice as powerful as the pleasure of gaining. This causes traders to hold onto losing positions too long hoping they break even, while cutting winning positions early.

## FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)
FOMO causes traders to enter positions late into extended price runs without proper setup confirmation or favorable risk-reward ratios.

## Rules for Disciplined Trading
1. Define entry, exit, and stop-loss before placing the trade.
2. Accept losses as a normal business expense.
3. Keep a detailed trading journal.
4. Take breaks after 2 consecutive losing trades.
