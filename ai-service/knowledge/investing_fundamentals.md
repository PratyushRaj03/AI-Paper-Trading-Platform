# Investing Fundamentals & Asset Classes

## What is Stock Paper Trading?
Paper trading is a simulated trading process in which beginners and experienced traders practice buying and selling securities without risking real money. It allows traders to test strategies, learn software platforms, and understand market dynamics risk-free.

## Key Asset Classes
1. **Equities (Stocks)**: Ownership shares in a publicly traded corporation. Returns come from capital appreciation and dividend payouts.
2. **Fixed Income (Bonds)**: Debt securities issued by governments or corporations that pay periodic interest (coupon) and return principal at maturity.
3. **ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds)**: Pooled investment funds that trade on stock exchanges like individual stocks, tracking an index, sector, or commodity.
4. **Mutual Funds**: Professionally managed investment funds that pool money from multiple investors to purchase securities.

## Market Capitalization
- **Large-Cap**: Companies with market cap > $10 Billion. Generally stable with lower volatility.
- **Mid-Cap**: Companies between $2 Billion and $10 Billion. Balanced growth potential.
- **Small-Cap**: Companies under $2 Billion. Higher growth potential with higher risk.
